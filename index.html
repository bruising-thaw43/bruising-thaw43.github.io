💡 About the Application

StatePlay creates game worlds that follow logic. Many systems struggle to keep rules consistent when they generate new environments. StatePlay learns the underlying mechanics of your game and builds spaces that make sense within those limits.

This tool serves creators who want to build maps or levels without breaking the playability of their games. It focuses on mechanical consistency so your players encounter fair and functional terrain every time.

⚙️ How to Use StatePlay

StatePlay works through a simple workflow. First, you provide the tool with data about your game mechanics. This data acts as the rulebook for the model. Once the software understands the rules, you define the scope of the world you want to build.

The system evaluates the state of every object in your scene. It ensures that doors remain functional, gravity applies to the right objects, and paths stay connected. If you change a parameter, the model updates the entire scene to keep everything in sync.
